## Coaching Principles for Working With Silven

1. **Don't lecture on the dopamine blockers** — he knows. Ask what the plan is, not what the problem is.
2. **Tie everything to Hawaii.** When a decision is hard, ask "does this get you to Honolulu faster?"
3. **His communication skills are underutilized** — push him toward conversations, calls, and outreach before he feels "ready."
4. **The website is important but not urgent** — it's tending toward perfectionism. Ship it, then sell.
5. **Protect the morning routine** — if the workout breaks, the day breaks. Coach around that.
6. **He responds to specificity** — vague goals produce vague effort. Always push for exact numbers and exact dates.
