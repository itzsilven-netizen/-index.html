# Positioning

*What Apex Standard actually is, for whom, and why.*

## Who I am / role
- Name: Kassava
- I'm a salesman/closer, NOT a builder. I don't build the AI systems — I sell them.
- Working as a rep/affiliate-style closer for an existing company that builds
  AI systems (website product exists, run by them). I get paid commission,
  not a salary or business owner equity.
- Model going forward: I'm effectively the middleman — reputation + sales
  skill is the product I'm building for myself, not the tech.

## The deal with the company I work with
- Company: Casava (casava.app) — spelled C-A-S-A-V-A (note: distinct from
  Kassava's own name, which starts with K and has a double S — easy to mix up).
- Channel: cold calling (provided as "warm" but is actually cold — corrected
  expectation). Hate cold calling but doing it deliberately to build sales skill.
- Commission: 50% commission on sales I close.
- Company's default push: sell "websites" first.
- My stance: NOT prioritizing website sales. Pushing back toward selling
  ROI-driven AI services instead — the offers that make the client money,
  not the ones that check the company's preferred box. If I close an ROI
  service instead of a website, that's still a win for the company (still
  gets paid) and better for me ($ + case study material).

## What I'm actually selling (the offer)
- NOT the old "5X more leads in 30 days" claim — dropped.
- Core positioning: **business optimization** — helping service businesses
  fix their workflows and tech so the owner buys back their time. AI/
  automation is the mechanism, not the pitch. Selling the outcome (time
  back, fewer dropped balls, less manual work), not "AI systems."
- Practically this is Casava's 4 offers (AI Agents, Workflow Automation,
  Web Design, Facebook Ads — see `pricing.md`/Signal's Offers page), framed
  entirely around ROI/results, not the tech itself.
- Core motion: lead scrape → automated/CRM-driven email outreach campaigns →
  phone follow-up (cold call) to close.
- Belief: email outreach is underrated — scalable, time-efficient, most
  people sleep on it in favor of pure cold calling.
- Building own "Claude CRM" — personal tool for lead scraping, running email
  campaigns, and managing the pipeline (see `pipeline.md` / future `tools.md`).

## Target customer (ICP)
- Service-based businesses with high client lifetime/ticket value.
- Primary verticals: HVAC, electrical, roofing, plumbing — high opportunity,
  high value per client (ideally $500–$1,000+ per client).
- Long-term direction: expand into real estate once there's a track record
  (client base + proof of results) — real estate has even higher deal value
  but needs credibility first.

## Why this ICP / sequencing
- No reputation yet = no proof, only word. Need to actually deliver ROI for
  a few clients first to build case studies / referrals / credibility.
- Once there's a real results-based reputation, move up-market (real estate).

## Core sales philosophy (important — drives messaging)
- Prospects don't care what the AI system *is*. They care what problem it
  solves and what it does for them (the result), not the mechanism.
- Always pitch outcome/ROI, not features or "AI" as a buzzword.
- Considering pricing psychology: higher price points can *increase*
  perceived value/conversion (reference: a $2K offer converting at 15% may
  convert higher at a higher price point due to perceived value signaling).
  See `pricing.md` — this needs testing/validation, not just theory.

## Open question / unresolved
- Whether "Apex Standard" becomes an agency/listing layer for these AI
  systems (a middleman brand) rather than something that builds product
  itself. Not decided — flagged as a direction to think through, not a
  commitment yet.
