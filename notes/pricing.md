# Pricing

- Base structure: using the company's existing pricing model as the floor —
  belief that these offers are underpriced and could sell for more.
- Commission: 50% on closed deals (paid by the company I work with).
- Pricing psychology to test: raising price can raise conversion, not just
  margin — psychological signal of higher value/quality at a higher price
  point (e.g. a hypothetical $2K offer at 15% close rate potentially
  converting better at a higher price due to perceived value). NOT yet
  validated with real numbers — treat as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.
